Privacy Policy — Capitals Trainer

Updated: June 22, 2026

Capitals Trainer is an app for learning the world’s capitals. It does not collect, transmit, or request any personal data.

What is stored

Your learning progress and chosen language are stored only on your device. This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us.

No collection or sharing

The app has no accounts, servers, ads, analytics, or third-party services. It does not use an internet connection.

Children

The app is safe for users of any age and collects no data.

Managing your data

You can reset your progress in Settings. Deleting the app permanently removes all stored data.
