Capitals Trainer — Support

Capitals Trainer is an iPhone app for learning the capitals of the world: flashcards, quizzes, maps, photos, and facts about every country. Available in English, Русский, Español, Français, and Deutsch.

Contact

Questions, feedback, or problems? Email us at alena.tomsk@gmail.com — we usually reply within a few days.

Frequently asked

How do I reset my learning progress?
Open Settings inside the app and tap “Reset progress”.

How do I change the language?
The study language can be changed in the app’s Settings.

Does the app need an internet connection?
No. Everything works fully offline.

Privacy

The app collects no data at all. See the Privacy Policy.
